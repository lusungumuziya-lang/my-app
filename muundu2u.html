Security & privacy
Muundo2U v1.0.2 · Lusungu Muziya
🔒
Bank-grade security
Enterprise-level protocols protecting every Zambian artist and subscriber on the platform.
JWT + OTP authentication
No passwords stored anywhere. SMS one-time codes via Africa's Talking API. JWT tokens expire in 24h. Refresh tokens: 30-day TTL in encrypted keychain only.
SSL certificate pinning
All API calls pinned against SHA-256 certificate fingerprints for api.muundo2u.zm and payments.muundo2u.zm. Man-in-the-middle attacks blocked at transport layer.
Biometric authentication
Face ID (iOS) and Fingerprint (Android) required on every session open. Fallback to OTP if biometrics unavailable. Powered by platform Keystore APIs.
AES-256 encrypted storage
Zero plain-text PII stored on device. Android Keystore system on Android. iOS Keychain (kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlocked) on iOS. All financial data encrypted at rest.
Root & jailbreak detection
App detects compromised devices on every launch and alerts users. Prevents keychain extraction attacks on rooted Android and jailbroken iOS devices.
ZRA Smart Invoice compliance
16% VAT + 2% MoMo Levy automatically deducted and remitted from every transaction. Smart Invoices generated for all payments and artist payouts. Monthly VAT returns filed automatically by 21st.
Certificate transparency
All Muundo2U TLS certificates logged to public CT logs. Protection against mis-issued certificates. Enforced natively on iOS (ATS) and verified programmatically on Android.
